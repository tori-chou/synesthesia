Color → Sound

Each color channel controls a different dimension of synthesis. Mix colors to combine effects.

Brightness → Volume Brighter paint plays louder.
Warm (R−B) → FM Depth Red/orange = buzzy, Blue/cyan = sine.
Green → Vibrato Green channel drives a 5.5 Hz LFO on pitch.

Palette guide

  • White — loud, no FM, max vibrato
  • Cyan — pure sine + strong vibrato
  • Green — quiet, no FM, max vibrato
  • Violet — no FM, subtle vibrato
  • Orange — warm FM + medium vibrato
  • Red — heavy FM, almost no vibrato
